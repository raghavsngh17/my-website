See the courses Why CodeForge

Built by developers who kept getting asked "where did you learn this?"

CodeForge started in 2023 as evening sessions run out of a co-working space, teaching three friends how to ship their first React app. Word spread, and the sessions turned into a proper course.

We keep every cohort small enough that an instructor can look at your code, not just your grade. Each course ends with a project you actually own: something you can put in a portfolio or a resume, not a certificate that sits in a folder.

Lead instructor

Aditi Rao

Nine years building web and mobile products, five of them mentoring junior developers on the job. Aditi designed the CodeForge curriculum around the mistakes she sees new developers make most often, and the habits that fix them.

A path, not a playlist

Take these in order and you'll go from your first line of code to a working app you built yourself. Each course builds directly on the last.

01

JavaScript Fundamentals

Variables, functions, arrays and the DOM, taught through eight small browser projects instead of slides.

4 weeks Beginner
02

Web Development Bootcamp

HTML, CSS and JavaScript combined into full pages, then connected to a backend and a real database.

8 weeks Beginner–Intermediate
03

React Mastery

Component thinking, state management and hooks, ending with a multi-page app deployed to the web.

6 weeks Intermediate
04

Android App Development

Build and publish a working Android app, from your first activity to a listing on the Play Store.

7 weeks Intermediate
