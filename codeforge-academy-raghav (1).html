Built by a developer who kept getting asked "where did you learn this?"

CodeForge is a small coding academy I run alongside my own work as a web development intern. It started as me helping classmates get unstuck on their first HTML pages, and turned into a proper set of courses.

Every cohort stays small enough that I can actually look at your code, not just your grade. Each course ends with a project you own: something real for your portfolio, not a certificate that sits in a folder.
